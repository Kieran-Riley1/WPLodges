Woodlands Park Lodges

Discover the charm of Woodlands Park Lodges. Set among peaceful farmland and woodland, both Chestnut and Cherry lodge offer a calm, tranquil space to enjoy the countryside.

Welcome

Comfort, space and stunning countryside views

Each lodge offers comfortable, bright and airy, open-plan living with stunning views from every window. You’ll find a spacious kitchen, dining and living area, three generous bedrooms and a family bathroom — perfect for families and groups of up to six.

Step outside to a secure decking area with a luxurious private hot tub and comfortable outdoor furniture — the perfect spot to unwind and soak up the calm, tranquil surroundings.

And because every lodge is step-free and easy to get around, with an en-suite wet room and adaptable equipment available on request, everyone can relax with ease — whatever your needs.

Our Lodges

Two unique lodges. Designed for everyone.

Two beautifully finished lodges with the same exceptional comfort — each with its own style, colour and character, and easy step-free access throughout.

Chestnut Lodge

Calm. Coastal. Pet friendly.

A luxurious retreat with bold teal accents and warm, contemporary styling. Spacious open-plan living opens onto an enclosed deck with a sunken private hot tub — and your dog is welcome too.

  • Sleeps up to 6 guests
  • 3 spacious bedrooms
  • Sunken private hot tub
  • Step-free & easy access
  • Pet friendly (1 dog)

Cherry Lodge

Warm. Bright. Inviting.

A luxurious retreat with a sunlit mustard-gold feature wall and warm, contemporary style. Bright open-plan living and a private hot tub on the decking — a relaxing, pet-free countryside base.

  • Sleeps up to 6 guests
  • 3 spacious bedrooms
  • Private hot tub
  • Step-free & easy access
  • Pet-free
